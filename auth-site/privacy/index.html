PRIVACY POLICY

Your data should work for you.

Effective August 12, 2026

This policy explains how Beyond Solid LLC (“Leafy,” “we,” or “us”) handles information when you use the Leafy iOS app.

Information we collect

We collect information you choose to provide, including account identifiers; body, goal, activity, and preference information; food, meal, nutrient, and weight records; check-ins and plan revisions; photos you submit for meal estimation; optional country or region information; and support communications.

Leafy does not currently collect HealthKit data, precise location, advertising identifiers, contacts, or payment information, and does not use third-party advertising trackers.

How we use information

  • Provide accounts, personalized targets, food logging, weight tracking, insights, reminders, and support.
  • Process meal descriptions and optional photos to estimate nutrition.
  • Protect Leafy, diagnose errors, prevent abuse, and comply with law.

No sale of health data

Leafy does not sell your nutrition, food, weight, or health information. We do not provide a commercial nutrition-data program or buyer-access dataset.

Sharing and service providers

Supabase provides authentication, database, storage, and server infrastructure. OpenAI may process meal descriptions, optional photos, and nutrition questions to provide AI features. Apple processes app distribution, crash reports, and feedback under Apple’s terms. We may disclose information when required by law, to protect safety and rights, or in a corporate transaction with appropriate safeguards.

Storage, security, and retention

Leafy uses encrypted network connections, account-scoped authorization, row-level database controls, and private storage. We retain active-account information as needed to provide Leafy. Account deletion requests removal of the account and associated plans, logs, weights, contribution records, and private media, subject to narrow legal or security obligations.

Your choices

You can edit records, change notifications, or delete your account in the app. For access, correction, or deletion help, email support@projectleafy.app.

Children and medical use

Leafy is intended for adults age 18 and older. It provides general wellness estimates and is not a medical device or a substitute for a clinician.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as Leafy changes and will revise its effective date. Questions can be sent to support@projectleafy.app.
